Treatment of infertility

Thus, we have our diagnosis, now it is time to take just one small step more – decide how we will induce pregnancy!
A lot of our future patients get stuck on this stage, especially if the doctor did not find anything alarming. This is fundamentally wrong! The failure to determine the reason behind infertility does not mean the doctor can let the couple go home, having told them "Just wait, it will all work out well". Patients try hard and keep their hopes high, and then finally show up for a consultation with a fertility specialist when we already have little to offer them.
All that, given that the options we are to suggest are already clearly identified!

It turns out, there are only four methods of infertility treatment:

1

Surgical treatment (laparoscopy, hysteroscopy)

2

Conservative treatment: controlled ovulation induction with/without insemination

3

Insemination in the natural cycle

4

IVF, IVF with donor oocytes, IVF with donor sperm, IVF with a surrogate mothe
