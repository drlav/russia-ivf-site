Step by step

An ultrasound of the pelvic organs

In the course of this investigation, a competent doctor will give you the respective information and will show you on the screen how many antral follicles he has managed to count. This is the reserve that gives us hope that there is yet a lot of time. Or it does not… You will be able to see for yourself how many black "holes" 2 to 10 mm in size there are in the ovaries and that "all is quiet on the western front". The ovarian reserve is considered normal when there are 6 to 20 follicles for both ovaries and diminished when there are less than 6. This is a situation that demands action. The presence of more than 20 follicles testifies to ovarian multifollicularity, but this is quite a different matter. Of course, aside from the ovaries, the uterus, the endometrium and the existence of cysts and myomas are to be evaluated as well, giving the doctor additional information that will help him/her to estimate your prognosis.
