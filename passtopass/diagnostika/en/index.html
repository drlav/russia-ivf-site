Diagnosis of reasons behind infertility

Diagnosis of any condition is the principal and most important step in the treatment of any disease. The first medical consultation, the talk with the doctor, the examination and the assessment of the need to undergo further investigations are the factors that determine how fast, how effectively and how most safely you will find your very own way towards the long-awaited pregnancy. Our experience suggests that it is exactly on this stage that patients waste a tremendous amount of time, health and nervous energy, often without having even started the major battle.
