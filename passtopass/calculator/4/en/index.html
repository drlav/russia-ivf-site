An estimated cost of the standard ivf programme

Let us try to calculate the cost of a cycle:
1. We have already settled the matter with Gonal F: we need two 900 IU pens and one 450 IU pen;
2. 3 Orgalutran 0,25 syringes;
3. 2 Pregnil 5000 IU vials;
4.3 VISITS aimed at SUPEROVULATION INDUCTION;
5. OVARIAN PUNCTURE, during which we have obtained 10 oocytes and sperm of not very good quality, which means that:
6. We perform ICSI of 5 to 12 oocytes;
7. We carry out the EMBRYO STAGE.
8. You come for an EMBRYO TRANSFER , maybe even for a SECOND EMBRYO TRANSFER. A fertility specialist may also perform HATCHING.
9. We have managed to VITRIFY 3 embryos and HAVE STORED them AWAY for THREE months.
