EMBRYOLOGY


Embryology is the heart, brain and soul of our department. These are the hands and the equipment that are used to fight for each and every oocyte, sperm cell and embryo. Without the high-quality work of our embryologists, all the efforts we put into choosing the right stimulation protocols and medications as well as into preparing the endometrium are reduced to zero. This is why quality control and audit operations are primarily conducted in these three small rooms. For us, as fertility specialists, the embryologists of our department are like sea captains resolutely steering our ship and promptly reacting to any changes in the life of your babies in our little nursery.
