BIOCHEMICAL PREGNANCY - Glossary from the field of reproductology, embryology and andrology

BIOCHEMICAL PREGNANCY

is a pregnancy determined only by the content of chorionic gonadotropin in the blood (or urine), synthesized by the cells of the embryo.
