Embryology Laboratory Audit

On the 26th of May, 2015 the Reproduction Department of EMC was audited for conformity to the ISO 9001:2008 (Quality Management) and ISO 15189:2007 (Laboratory Processes) standards.

On the 26th of May, 2015 the Reproduction Department of EMC was audited for conformity to the ISO 9001:2008 (Quality Management) and ISO 15189:2007 (Laboratory Processes) standards. The leading auditor was CECILIA SJOBLOM, a Professor at the University of Sydney and Scientific Director at Westmead Fertility Centre.
