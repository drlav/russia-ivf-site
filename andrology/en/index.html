ANDROLOGY

Andrology is a field of Medicine dealing with male reproductive system diseases and methods for their treatment. The main difference between Andrology and General Urology is that Andrology focuses on such issues as male infertility, hypogonadism, male contraception, male sexual dysfunction, obesity and ageing issues in men. In Europe, Andrology has long been an independent branch of Medicine, but in Russia andrologists are often urologists who have a better idea of sexually transmitted infections, kidney stone disease and developmental abnormalities of sex organs. Many think that the problems troubling our male patients and, most importantly, greatly affecting the efficacy of fertility specialists' work are just minor trifles. Which is why finding your own "men's doctor" who, together with a fertility specialist, will guide you and your partner through the uneasy task of getting a baby is a great piece of luck.
